# Credits
All art and audio of this mod was generated for it with fal (nano-banana-2 images, z-image turbo tiling textures, ElevenLabs sound effects and voice); no files from other games.
- Neon ad signs, street floor, hazard wall and ceiling textures: generated with fal, original.
- Sound effects (hack, sandevistan, cab honk, eddies, boss stinger) and Johnny voice lines: generated with fal, original.
- Portal 2 models, particles and sounds (turrets, personality sphere, companion cube, glass panels) are reused from the installed game, not redistributed.
- "Cyberpunk 2077" is only the theme; no assets, names beyond generic terms, logos or code from it are used.
