# Cyberpunk 2077 Takeover

**Portal 2** × Cyberpunk 2077 · visual · idea by the team

Bring Cyberpunk 2077 into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned a Portal 2 test chamber into Night City: neon billboards, Johnny hacking turrets, and a Delamain cab cube flung through a portal.

## Install

Copy the mod folder's contents (scripts, materials, sound, resource and the pak01 files) into a new folder named sigf inside your Portal 2 folder, then start Portal 2 with the launch options -game sigf +sv_cheats 1 +map sp_a2_catapult_intro.
